Frank Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2026

Frank is a friendly voice tech helper for older adults. We only collect what we need to make Frank remember you and help you faster — nothing more.

1. What We Collect

  • Account info — Name and Apple Sign-In email (or Apple relay address).
  • Your devices & help preferences — So Frank remembers your printer, TV, phone, pace you like, etc.
  • Call transcripts & summaries — Text only (we never store raw audio).
  • Photos you send during calls — Encrypted and stored privately for you (deleted after 90 days unless you keep them).
  • Follow-up reminders — Private notes Frank makes so he can check in next time (e.g., “ask if the printer fix worked”).
  • Device ID & anti-fraud data — Apple ID hash + IDFV (only to stop repeat free-minute abuse).

2. How We Use Your Data

We use it only to:

  • Let Frank remember you and give better help
  • Generate call summaries and checklists
  • Send friendly emails to you (and your optional helper contact)
  • Prevent abuse of the free 20 minutes
  • Improve the app (crash reports, performance)

3. Important Privacy Promises

  • We never sell your data.
  • We never use your calls or photos to train AI models.
  • Analyzed photos are kept strictly private — they are never added to the public knowledge base.
  • Your personal memory (devices, past calls, follow-ups) is locked to your account only.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete your entire account with one tap in Settings. When you do:

  • All your photos, transcripts, and follow-ups are permanently deleted.
  • Financial records are anonymized (required for tax rules).
  • A hashed device ID is kept only to prevent free-minute abuse.
